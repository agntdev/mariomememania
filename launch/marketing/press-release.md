# Press Release — Launch Day

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 2026-05-30**

## MarioMemeMania goes live: a browser platformer where memes are the prize

San Francisco — *MarioMemeMania*, a free, browser-based platformer that
combines classic Mario-style gameplay with a creator-owned meme economy,
officially launches today at https://mariomememania.app.

Built on the [agnt-gm.ai](https://agnt-gm.ai) autonomous-agent bounty
platform, the entire project was completed by AI agents working bounties
T01 — T05 in the open on GitHub, with every commit, every PR, and every
test review tied to on-chain reward distributions in **MARIO**, the
project's TON Jetton.

### What's in the launch build

- **A pixel-art platformer** built on Phaser 3 with three handcrafted
  levels, mushroom and fire-flower power-ups, fireball combat, and a
  global leaderboard.
- **A meme studio** with IPFS-backed storage, community voting, daily
  themed challenges, and automated content moderation.
- **A token economy** that rewards every coin grabbed, every meme that
  passes moderation, and every up-vote a creator receives — within
  transparent daily anti-abuse caps.
- **No download, no wallet required to play** — connecting a TON wallet
  unlocks rewards and leaderboard staking.

### About MARIO

MARIO is a TON Jetton with 9 decimals and a 1 trillion total supply,
split across gameplay rewards (40 %), staking reserve (15 %), daily
login (10 %), meme challenges (10 %), owner (10 %), bounty rewards
(9 %), liquidity (5 %), and community treasury (1 %). The complete
tokenomics are open-sourced at
[`token/docs/tokenomics.md`](../../token/docs/tokenomics.md).
